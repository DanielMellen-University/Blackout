Game info

  • Spawn on a flat airfield. Spool the engine, build speed, then rotate.
  • Throttle is thrust. A climb spends speed, and closing the throttle slows you down. Afterburner is a short burst, not a cruise setting.
  • Fly the glowing rings in order. The cue at the top points at the next gate.
  • A sortie may ask for a clean circuit, a low pass, a centered landing, a soft landing, or, rarely, a deadstick glide.
  • Land to score the run. A crash or ditch opens the same card: time, gates cleared, and the score.
  • R rolls a new world. Pause or the results card can retry the same course.

Blackout is a browser arcade F-35 toy. Take off, fly the gates, and land or crash.
