THE SHORT VERSION

Power. Climb.
Find your line.

This is an arcade jet. Point the nose where you want to fly, manage your speed, and leave room to recover.
01 / FIRST FLIGHT

Get off the runway.

  1. Pick Training Orbit. It is the easiest place to learn the gate-and-return loop.
  2. Raise power and roll straight. Hold Shift / 2, or the right trigger. Release it when the engine command is where you want it.
  3. Pitch up gently once moving. Use the pitch-up direction shown on the Controls page; avoid a steep climb immediately after liftoff.
  4. Build a little height. Gear retracts automatically once clear of the ground unless you have manually overridden it.

Start in chase view. You can see your bank angle and the space around the jet. Use C to change views.

02 / HANDLING & ENERGY

The nose is your flight path.

Turn with intent

Roll sets your bank; pitch moves the nose in that banked plane. Combine them for a sweeping turn, then roll back toward level. Yaw nudges the nose left or right for corrections.

Keep some speed in reserve

Throttle changes thrust, not instant speed. Climbing spends speed; descending builds it. Ease the climb or add power if the jet starts to sag.

Afterburner is a burst of extra thrust, not a separate throttle setting. Hold it when you need acceleration, then release it. Heat and fuel can limit it. For a quick slowdown, reduce throttle and hold the brake.

Flight assist trims pitch and bank when those inputs are released. It is not autopilot and will not steer you through gates.

03 / INSTRUMENTS

Read what matters.

IAS / KT
Your current airspeed in knots. The target marker shows the speed associated with the current engine command-not a cruise-control setting.
ENGINE / FUEL
Commanded engine power, afterburner availability, and remaining fuel. Long boosts spend fuel and build heat.
ALT / VERTICAL SPEED
Height above the local surface and your climb or descent rate. Rising terrain can reduce clearance even while you are climbing.
HEADING / ATTITUDE
The compass shows direction. The attitude display shows pitch and bank relative to the horizon.
NAVIGATION
Target direction and distance. A target behind you needs a turn, not a steeper climb. A selected city or village is not a mission gate.

First person is deliberately unobstructed: no cockpit frame or instruments. Use an external view when you need to check the flight deck.

04 / THE MISSION

Out through the gates. Back to base.

A mission is one flight attempt. A course defines the route and conditions. Follow the navigation cue and fly through the gates in order; smooth, centred crossings improve gate quality. After the final gate, follow the base cue home and land to finish.

TAKE OFF→CLEAR GATES→RETURN & LAND

Read the selected course’s goal and contract before launch. Medals and personal bests reward repeat attempts. The ghost shows your saved best-run path when available; toggle it with X.

05 / RETURN & LAND

Give the approach some room.

  1. Line up early. Approach along the runway rather than turning sharply over it. Reduce power well before touchdown.
  2. Lower the gear. Use your configured gear key and check its state. Gear also lowers automatically near the ground.
  3. Keep a shallow descent. Use small corrections. The runway lights show two white / two red when you are on the intended glide path; more white means high, more red means low.
  4. Level the wings and ease the descent. Flare gently near the surface. Landing too fast, on a steep slope, or with a hard sink can crash the jet.
  5. Brake after contact. Hold your brake key to slow the rollout.

If the approach is rushed, add power, climb clear, and make another circuit. Water contact is a crash, not a landing.

Refuelling at base

After flying, return to the airfield, stop with gear down, and close the throttle. Fuel replenishes while you remain stopped in the base refuelling area.

06 / EXPLORATION & RETRIES

Keep the world. Or find another.

Infinite World is for exploring terrain, waterways, and settlements without a gate circuit. Use radar targets to find landmarks. Authored courses can lock their weather; N changes weather only when the course allows it.

Same course

Pause → Retry restarts the current mission. At results or during the crash cinematic, Enter retries the same course.

New world

R ends the flight and generates a new world. To revisit a world, copy its replay link with Y or enter its seed on the launch screen.

Crashes show a brief cinematic, then your flight report. Water impacts have their own splash. Progress, records, favourites, and best-run ghosts are stored locally in this browser.
