How to play

  • You spawn on a flat airfield. Spool the engine, build speed, then rotate.
  • Stay airborne, explore biomes, and watch the day/night cycle and weather.
  • Crash? Press R for a new world and runway.
  • Cycle weather with N. Switch cameras with C.
