Game info

  • Spawn on a flat airfield. Spool the engine, build speed, then rotate.
  • Fly the glowing cyan rings in order. The top arrow points at the next gate.
  • Generated gate legs are checked against the terrain corridor, so the intended line stays flyable between checkpoints.
  • Takeoff: Shift to add power, then W at speed. G toggles gear, with automatic low-altitude safety.
  • On final approach, the caution row shows `FLARE` in the short gear-down landing window so you know when to ease the sink rate.
  • If the approach becomes unstable, the same row shows `GO AROUND` so you can add power and try the landing again.
  • Some seeded sorties assign `CROSSWIND`; hold the existing runway-relative wind long enough to complete the task.
  • Some seeded sorties assign `G CONTROL`; keep speed up while staying inside the safe G envelope.
  • Some seeded sorties assign `DEADSTICK`; let the tank run dry in the air, then glide home and land.
  • Some seeded sorties assign `FRONT CHASER`; stay airborne while the current weather front shifts.
  • After the circuit, return and land to score. R rolls a new world. Pause or results can retry the same course.
  • Choose Random world, Free flight, or a repeatable course seed before launch. New world applies the selected course.
  • Engine percent is a speed target: 50% holds about 1500 kts. Afterburner needs throttle open.
  • Press T to lock the next radar settlement. The navigation cue guides you there until you cycle again.
  • Press Y during flight to copy a replay link. Opening it restores the same procedural world seed.
  • Chain clean gates and barrel rolls within 8 seconds to build combo rewards and a capped score bonus.
  • Seeded sorties show a TASK row with live bonus-contract progress, including low-pass terrain-hugger and biome-tour challenges, then mark it DONE when you meet the objective.
  • Touch down with fuel remaining for a small efficiency bonus; afterburner speed is still worth the burn when you need it.
  • Center the jet on the home strip and match its heading for a small approach bonus.
  • Clean landings through rain, snow, or strong gusts earn a capped weather-handling bonus.
  • Replay curated courses to beat their saved approach record and sharpen your return.
  • Try Coastal run for a lower sweeping route that follows changing terrain and shoreline relief.
  • Try River run for a low meandering line through inland water country.
  • Repeat a curated course in external view to race the faint best-run ghost path; the trace stays local and is replaced only by a higher score.
  • The live GHOST row compares your elapsed time with that saved trace and stays hidden when no best run exists.
  • Land centered and aligned with a safe touchdown to earn the APPROACH ACE mastery badge.
  • Use T to lock a radar settlement and reach it for a capped destination bonus before returning home.
  • A SETTLEMENT TOUR contract may ask you to visit both a generated city and a village, turning radar targets into a route.
  • The SETTLEMENT TOUR task line marks each tier OPEN or OK as you arrive, so the remaining destination is always clear.
  • A COMBO RUN contract may ask you to build a three-link chain from clean gates and barrel rolls before the chain expires.
  • The COMBO RUN task line keeps the current chain milestone visible as the separate combo row fades on compact HUD presets.
  • A PRECISION CHAIN contract may ask for three consecutive high-quality gate clears; one rough pass resets that chain without ending the sortie.
  • Fly across different terrain provinces to build a bounded biome survey record and exploration bonus.
  • The live SURVEY row counts distinct biomes, and each new province gets one calm discovery cue.
  • Push through Mach 1 for a single restrained sonic-boom cue; speed jitter will not retrigger it.
  • The cockpit MACH row shows subsonic, transonic, or supersonic speed at a glance.
  • An ENERGY BAND contract may ask you to hold a steady cruise window for a short airborne stretch.
  • A STORM RUN contract may ask you to stay airborne in meaningful rain or snow for a short stretch.
  • A PRECISION APPROACH contract may ask for a centered, aligned touchdown on the home strip.
  • Looping or pitching vertical keeps the last reliable heading on the HUD and return cue instead of flipping the navigation arrow.
  • The external camera shortens its chase line before loaded buildings and airfield structures instead of clipping through them.
  • The orbit camera gives a wide, horizon-stable view for reading the jet and terrain at speed.
  • The BRAKE CHECK contract rewards opening the speed brake above 428 knots while airborne, making energy control a deliberate choice.
  • The THERMAL CONTROL contract rewards holding engine heat below 72% at cruise speed, making afterburner timing matter.
  • A MACH RUN contract may ask you to stay airborne above Mach 1 for a short stretch, turning supersonic speed into a deliberate target.
  • A CLEAN CIRCUIT contract may ask you to clear every gate without a miss; an off-ring crossing marks that task failed while the sortie can continue.
  • Missing a gate on CLEAN CIRCUIT announces the contract failure once, while the rest of the sortie remains recoverable.
  • A LEVEL FLIGHT contract may ask you to hold a steady altitude band for a short airborne stretch; drifting too far resets that progress.
  • The TERRAIN HUGGER contract uses radio altitude above the rendered ground, so ridges and valleys change the low-pass challenge honestly.
  • Keyboard yaw direction is configurable in Settings and persists between sessions; gamepad and touch yaw remain unchanged.
  • Keyboard roll direction is configurable in Settings and persists between sessions; gamepad and touch roll remain unchanged.
  • Keyboard pitch direction is configurable in Settings and persists between sessions; gamepad and touch pitch remain unchanged.
  • Hard positive G tunnels vision into a dark vignette; strong negative G adds a restrained red wash with one BLACKOUT or REDOUT cue.
  • Try the Ridge run course for a high-altitude gate line over the mountain shoulders.

Blackout is a browser arcade F-35 toy. Manage fuel, chase radar contacts, and just fly.
