Game info

  • Spawn on a flat airfield. Spool the engine, build speed, then rotate.
  • Fly the glowing cyan rings in order. The top arrow points at the next gate.
  • Takeoff: Shift to add power, then W at speed. Gear is automatic.
  • Crash or land, then R for a new world and circuit.
  • Engine percent is a speed target: 50% holds about 500 kts.

Blackout is a browser arcade F-35 toy. No fuel, radar, or weapons. Just fly.
