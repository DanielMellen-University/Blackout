## Part D, 10.974

All six additional flights pass: both themes on seeds 1337, 2026 and 9001. Each launches through the DOM from its generated runway, flies all five gates with production physics, observes boost and five ordinary 25% nitro refill calls, touches down inside the runway footprint, and reaches the existing landed/complete report with gold. No pose, velocity, orientation, gate, clock or completion writes are used. Paired chase/cockpit stills are inspected; low approach and terminal stills are also retained under d/. The pilot and all runtime source hashes are checked by `node scripts/summarize-generator.mjs`; only release identity may advance after capture. Normal production bundles have no review hook.

See [the measured results](completion-results.md) and [strict machine summary](completion-summary.json). Full-cadence mean FPS ranges from 41.6 to 48.2; p95 is 33.4 ms in all six runs. Counts above 50 ms are 2, 20 and 16 for desert, and 19, 14 and 14 for valley. Each run has one interval above 100 ms, with worst intervals of 349.9 to 383.3 ms. The frame record includes camera switches and still capture, so it does not establish the cause of those spikes or certify stutter-free flight. No controlled before/after performance improvement is claimed. Device scale is 1 at a 1280x720 viewport with Balanced and the unchanged adaptive scaler. No local test suite runs during these measurements.

Resident coverage has zero sampled misses after ten seconds in a 200 m square around the aircraft. Near-field LOD remains resident during the course, while stream pressure and loaded counts stay recorded in the receipts. A focused geometry check compares shared near/far shoulder samples, checks finite attributes on the immense mountains, and reuses the normal pooled buffers across a theme switch and return. Main-thread JS heap sampled peaks are 66.1 to 92.8 MB; this is not total browser or GPU memory and is not a long-flight heap certification. No heap tuning or budget reduction is included.

The defects found while integrating the batch were fixed before final capture: fractional seed replay/record isolation and generated record retention in B, and same-seed cross-theme settlement cache reuse in C. No further production bug was reproduced in Part D. FlightModel, flightConfig, NitroSystem, Collision, WaterSystem and RenderQuality are unchanged from 10.970. The paused systems and all non-goals stay excluded. Contour circuit, Weather approach, Training orbit and the old catalog keep their existing behavior and await later overhaul.

Final verification includes 79 focused checks across generator/LOD, catalog/session, records, original missions, b20 physics, b21 nitro, paused effects and version identity, plus the production build. The earlier full two-worker repeat passes 170 files / 1,672 checks, and CI is green on the pre-report 10.973 runtime. Final tip CI is checked after the 10.974 push.
