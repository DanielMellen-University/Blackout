## Reproduced defect and fix

The baseline at 6f26e7b puts gate 1 at 1,951.264 m, 1,779 m above the spawn and only about 1.13 km ahead. The route builder asks for 120 m of clearance at every sample, including the first sample after the grounded spawn. Solving that requirement back to the gate endpoint magnifies it by the sample count, yielding a roughly 57.6 degree first leg. The saved baseline controls pilot overshoots gate 1 while climbing and remains incomplete. This is a route-generation defect, not evidence that no expert pilot could ever complete the old course.

Departure now ramps its clearance from zero at the ground spawn to the full 120 m over the lesser of 800 m and the first-leg length. A 2 m departure reserve covers interpolation between the same fixed probes. Gate-to-gate legs keep their full 120 m requirement, three lateral corridor probes and existing sample cap. Validation checks every departure probe against the ramp, expressing its margin on the same 120 m scale; the advertised full corridor clearance applies after departure. No terrain/contact, water, obstacle, physics, resource or rendering budget changes are made. This is a generation-time route change, with no new per-frame feature.

The final Training gate 1 is 178.294 m above spawn, about 9.0 degrees uphill. Its X/Z and crossing direction are unchanged. Gates 2 through 5 retain all their original positions.
