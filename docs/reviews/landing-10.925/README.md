## Method and evidence

`scripts/review-landings.mjs` injects the external `browser-landing-review.js` into Vite's transformed main module. It prescribes an inbound trajectory before each actual physics step, then lets production physics, terrain resolution, collision, HUD, runway lights, scoring and rendering execute. This is scripted approach verification, not manual handling or a controls-only autopilot. Training gates are pre-completed through the challenge API to enter the return phase. Gate flight itself is not claimed.

Three independently generated airfields, seeds 42, 73 and 1337, are approached in both Free flight and Training Orbit. All six final records show safe ground contact. Seed 1337 classifies as `roll` within the contact tolerance before an impact, with no failure reason; Training Orbit correctly banks that returning contact and completes. The script checks actual HUD versus world guidance, base target, rendered pixel ratio, and nose/track probes inside and outside the final cone. Each run also directly probes -31, -30, -29.99, 0, 29.99, 30 and 31 degrees for both nose and track.

`all-six.json` combines the four dry-route records in `after.json` and the two shore-aware records in `shore-final.json`, preserving each hook hash. These files record source hashes, the hook hash, sampled routes, production lens colors, HUD values, boundary probes, physical contact and final challenge state. Final paired chase/cockpit stills use the actual production cameras at 1280 x 720 and pixel ratio 1, near the threshold after 18 simulation seconds. The five-second polling cadence means the saved pose is approximately 20 seconds into the approach. The uncluttered cockpit view intentionally hides the overlay, as in the production game. The three Training Orbit debrief captures use the same desktop viewport.

The inherited `before.json` proves the Free flight mismatch on seed 42: four white PAPI lenses and `high` world guidance, but no HUD glide cue. The earlier Training Orbit pilot already shows matching guidance and a working debrief. Earlier captures and incomplete runs remain under `pilot/`; one early-view run was invalidated by a metadata-triggered development reload and is not final evidence. The final review runs with source files frozen.

## Findings and limits

Runway centerline and inbound nose/track agree in the reviewed poses. The PAPI pattern correctly changes from high to two-white/two-red on the intercepted glide path. Generated relief can rise above the nominal PAPI slope farther out, particularly on seeds 42 and 1337. The prescribed route therefore remains above resident terrain before intercepting the final slope. The seed-1337 pilot also exposed a fixture defect: sampling the lake bed without the water surface commanded a sharp dive. The final shore-aware script clears resolved water and bounds its prescribed descent to a 0.25 height/distance gradient. Its PAPI remains correctly high on the steeper short approach. Failed fixture attempts remain under `pilot/`. A high indication in that region is truthful; this review does not certify terrain clearance along an extended constant-angle approach on every seed. Near threshold the PAPI corridor ends and the cue disappears before rollout, as designed.

The inspected desktop debrief fits its screen, exposes the landing assessment and coaching, and keeps retry/new-world actions visible. Free flight remains unscored exploration and does not open a mission-completion debrief. Landing physics, altitude/load telemetry, collision, terrain/water, budgets, landscape revision 28 and saved records are unchanged. Vegetation, blackout/redout effects and the flight-warning strip remain removed and paused.

The seven focused files pass 167 checks and the production build passes. Physical-device controls, manual flare/handling, full Training gate flights, crosswind handling and universal airfield clearance remain outside this verification.
