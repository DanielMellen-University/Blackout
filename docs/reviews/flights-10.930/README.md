These are real-physics flights under an external controls-only pilot, not manual play. There is one airborne reset before measurement. After that the pilot changes pitch, roll, yaw, throttle and assist only; it never prescribes subsequent positions, attitudes or velocities. Production physics, terrain/obstacle contact, automatic weather, fuel, HUD and streaming continue normally. The original three-kilometre terrain lookahead was insufficient on the first ridge flight. Shorter city/coast pilots also crash after 478.5 and 313.2 seconds and remain in `pilot-city/` and `pilot-coast/`. The exact contact classifications of those shorter pilots were not captured. The city/coast retries use eight-kilometre lookahead and more clearance. This is not proof that the scripted pilot or manual handling is universally safe.

`before/` contains the three qualifying runs and all 33 minute stills. `summary.json` validates their durations, cadence, image dimensions, runtime mode, seed, ratio and absence of application/shader errors. `hooks/` preserves each exact pilot source, with hashes matching the records. `served-source.json` identifies the frozen cb51f2f game served on port 5174 throughout the flights. Concurrent visual/test work makes these runs unsuitable for FPS or heap acceptance claims.

## Five most visible selected problems and fixes

1. **Floating city before terrain arrives.** The city pilot's initial view shows a whole settlement against empty sky. The reproduction finds 1,196 visible building positions with no resident terrain. Settlement presentation now waits for exact quadtree coverage across its footprint. Existing resident parents keep coverage valid while finer tiles replace them, preventing a new LOD visibility blink. There are no height probes, generated tiles, extra resources, collision changes or admission/distance reductions in the production coverage query. The reproduced early view has no floating settlement; the later view restores it once terrain is ready. Standalone hosts without a terrain-tree callback retain a bounded support check.
2. **Oversized polygon stars.** The seed-73 night stills show dense square and triangular white cells. The actual sky shader now uses sparse, round, antialiased angular points. The isolated pixel regression retains 47 points; mean lit component area falls from 9.40 to 3.38 pixels and the maximum from 51 to 6. Both isolated renders compile without errors. This changes the night shader's arithmetic and appearance; no performance win is claimed.
3. **Glowing clouds at night.** Nearby basic-material clouds remained far brighter than the surrounding night deck. Their illumination now follows daylight down to a low night tint, including cirrus. In the recorded clear-cloud fixture, the maximum linear material channel falls from .53 to .0795 while daylight readability remains covered by tests. Lightning remains an independent local effect.
4. **Narrow fair-weather columns.** Ordinary cumulus retained tall stacked centres even without convection. The centres now form a broader, shorter crown while individual billows retain their rounded scale. Full convection retains the original tower-centre envelope. The eight-formation fixture's maximum height/span ratio falls from 1.117 to .919, with the same 320-puff capacity. The existing rounded-billow and storm-shape tests still pass.
5. **Cruise exhaust reading as afterburner.** At 45 percent power with boost off, the long, broad plume dominated several chase stills. The dry-power envelope is now compact and narrower. The actual-object regression bounds its scale below .35 in length and .65 in width at that lever setting, while full afterburner still exceeds 2.8 in length. Engine thrust, heat, fuel and HUD power remain unchanged.

`issues-before.json` and `issues-after.json` reproduce four production-camera poses from the original flight samples. All eight paired stills are visually reviewed. The city has early and settled views; seed 73 supplies night and daylight views. These are static reproductions for comparison, clearly separate from the controls-only endurance flights. Their terrain/weather initialization can settle differently from a continuously travelled world, so they are not pixel-identical replays. `sky-before/after` isolate the actual production star shader. `cloud-metrics.json` records material and formation measurements. The star pixel check is automated by `scripts/check-sky-points.py`.

## Verification and remaining limits

The 14 affected files pass 199 tests, including actual coverage holes, overlapping LOD parents, unchanged settlement collision/plans, cloud shapes/lighting, aircraft exhaust and removed-system checks. The long weather-only lifecycle check initially exceeded Vitest's default five seconds while other browsers ran; the final affected set passes with a 15-second test timeout and unchanged assertions. Metadata/README checks and the production build pass. The existing bundle-size advisory remains.

A separate settled coarse-LOD foundation mismatch remains open: one distant seed-1337 lot has a centre/base separation of 20.09 m in both paired scans. The new readiness check fixes absent terrain, not those sampled ground heights. Coarse shoreline steps, independent wet-edge water stitching, universal road/town placement, all continuous popping, manual controls, complete gate flights and physical-device handling are not certified by this review. No new flight-warning strip, vegetation or blackout/redout effects are introduced. Landscape revision 29, scene detail, render distances, draw/instance/worker budgets and saved progress remain unchanged.
