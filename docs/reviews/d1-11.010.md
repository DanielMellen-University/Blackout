# d1 landing removal, 11.010 — Oct 26 2026

Approved scope: ROADMAP 6.1 and 6.2 together. Every gated mission now commits
its result at the final accepted gate, after the final gate combo and nitro
refill. Time, telemetry, ghost and Ops completion use that airborne finish.
There is no return phase, gated base navigation or landing preview. Free flight
keeps its optional runway guidance and open, unscored session.

The public release stays v0.12.0; internal chunk advances 11.009 → 11.010.

## Score budget

| Focus | Old gate / time / landing | New gate / time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Balanced | 20k / 70k / 10k | 30k / 70k |
| Gates | 30k / 60k / 10k | 40k / 60k |
| Pace | 18k / 76k / 6k | 24k / 76k |
| Legacy landing | 18k / 60k / 22k | 40k / 60k |

Remove approach (up to 500), weather-landing (500), night-landing (500) and
deadstick-landing (1,500) bonuses. No new run awards touchdown quality or
landing points. Other bonuses, the 117,500 ceiling, 320-point-per-second
overrun deduction, time allowances and minimum time scores are retained.
Bronze/silver/gold thresholds remain 64k/76k/88k. Historical records and their
existing identities remain readable; a separate record revision is later work.

The medal test covers all four focuses and 1–100 gates, each with bronze,
silver and gold flights: 80% gate quality, 50% fuel, no contract/stunt/streak
bonuses, and 80/45/10 seconds over the route allowance. The resulting score
ranges are 66.9–70.1k, 78.1–81.3k and 89.3–92.5k respectively.
